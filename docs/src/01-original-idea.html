1. The hackathon, in the organisers' words

IDEA//FORGE is an online hackathon hosted on Challazo (challazo.in). The brief is to build an AI-powered solution for a real problem: pick one of two tracks, choose a problem statement, and build.

The track I chose

Track 1: Civic Pulse, Digital Governance & Public Information. Make public information easier to find, government services easier to use, and policies easier to understand. You can use Generative AI, image and audio analysis, browser automation and AI agents.

The problem statement I chose — exact specification

2. Transparent Civic Grievance & Infrastructure Triage System. A platform where citizens report problems like damaged roads, pollution or water leaks using photos, videos or audio. It rates how serious each problem is, sends it to the right authority, and shows a public dashboard of complaints and fixes.

What the organisers ask of every builder

  • Pick a problem you understand and care about.
  • Make sure you can explain who faces the problem and how your AI helps.
  • Understand the problem clearly and build a solution that actually solves it.
  • Use AI in a meaningful way, not just as decoration.
  • Test your solution before submitting, and be ready to explain how your project works.

3. Why the name Thambi

I'm going to name this platform Thambi because there's a Tamil phrase which means, if you tell that boy, he will take care of it. It's a very common phrase in the Tamil household, where people trust their younger sons or their elder sons to take care of it if something goes wrong in the house. For plumbing work or electrical work, they trust their sons to do that. That's why we are doing this right now.

I pretty much like the phrase, so that's why I'm simply keeping it, because I like it.

4. The problem I live with

If I'm traveling every Sunday somewhere, that route, specifically the road, is pretty bad, because they've dug up the road for the metro water, putting in the pipe and gas pipes. For the metro train, they are putting in a pillar, and they're digging up the road, but the road is not maintained. It is not rebuilt and maintained pretty well.

Especially during the rainy seasons, it's pretty tough. You don't know where a pothole is, where you'll probably fall and die, so it's a nightmare going on the road, whether it's morning, evening, or night.

5. My idea

This is a platform where citizens can report problems like damaged roads, pollution, or water leaks using photos and videos. It rates how serious each problem is, sends it to the right authority, and shows a public dashboard of complaints and fixes. I want it to be a website.

What a citizen does

If there's a platform called Thambi, I'm going to just take a video. I'm going to explain what's wrong with it and how long this problem has been very persistent there. I'm going to take relevant photos and videos and then post it there. I'm going to write complaints by tagging the name of the road and its locality to identify its nearby location.

What the AI agent does

The AI agent in the platform is going to analyze the text complaint that the user has put in, and it is going to rate the severity of it. The video is mainly for visual evidence, so the agent is going to extract the problem and the details of the street where it is. It'll use the maps, locate it, and find that exact spot. Then it is going to search whether other people have reported the same problem.

The citizen's own severity check

In the user report form, we will give a severity check for them, because, as people, the citizens know which is severe and which is not severe. They've been traveling all their life here and there ever since they were born, so they know the severity to some level. They can do it right, so we can give them that severity check over there in the form.

6. Features

6.1 Find out if someone already reported it

The agent searches whether other people have reported the same problem. Person 1 has already reported a problem, and person 2 is about to report the same problem now. Person 2 can vote and support person 1, because both of them have the same problem, right? One person has already reported, and the other person could support it and strengthen his voice.

6.2 Voting, like a YouTube like

We can have a small vote button like the likes on the YouTube website. Like that, we can make it visible.

6.3 Ranking

If many people have reported, we can keep a ranking system where many people are reporting this problem in such a locality. These things should be segregated by categories: locality and severity. Keep these two things as the categories for splitting the things. We will use these two criteria for now. If there are more, tell me what categories we have to pick and rank them.

6.4 Sending it to the right authority

The AI agent inside the website should show how serious each problem is, and it should send it to the right authority. Based upon the severity, the problem should be sent to the right authority: higher-ranked, higher-severity problems first, and then, accordingly, lower-ranking problems.

6.5 Comments

People can also comment under another person's post. There would be a higher-severity problem and a lower-severity problem. For a higher-severity problem, one or two people would have voted for it, but for a lower-severity problem, many people are voting. This means people can go in the comment section and write, "We are facing this problem very much. Please look into it. Even though it's tagged as low severity, please do look into it. It will improve our daily livelihoods." Like that, people can comment, and then it will help them.

We can also give them some example comments — a prompt over there — so they can strengthen the voice of the other person who has the same problem as them.

6.6 Public dashboard of complaints and fixes

It should show a public dashboard of complaints and fixes. This should be very clear. Most of the people using it would be from young people to even old people, right? It should be easy for everyone to navigate. The public dashboard should be as simple as scrolling on Facebook, and it should show complaints as well as fixes.

7. Ideas

The AI agent that pops out while you type

If people want to report a problem that other people have already reported, when they start typing in the report form inside the website, the AI agent should pop out. There will be a small text box in the form, somewhere as the third or fourth question, in which the people will be typing. The AI agent should immediately recognize the text and say:

"There are similar problems in the same area which you are about to report. Do you want to fully type and let me pull out the exact problem, or do you want to go through the problem and vote on it?"

8. To be developed

8.1 A designer report form

We need to create a designer form for them in the right way, strategically, so that we can actually understand what their problem is from the form itself. We will strategically design it so that we can extract what the problem is from that report form, and the videos are for visual evidence.

8.2 The authority database

We have lots of data about the authorities all over the internet. I want to go and find the right data and maintain a database, a collection of it, so each problem goes to the right authority.

8.3 Scale and security

From my technical perspective, let's scale it to the first 100 users, so we stay inside the free limits when we deploy it on Vercel. Let us use a secure database, so no other person can come and change it.

9. Add-on features

9.1 Nobody can tamper with it

No other person can hack into the website, or come into the website and change something that is already fixed. Another person shouldn't come in and change something that is not fixed either.

9.2 Catching the fake and the abusive

Identify fake complaints or fake comments, people being abusive in the comments feature that we have, people falsely voting, or some AI bot activity in this. Detecting this should be there.

10. Decisions I've already made

  • A website, not an app. The app can crash sometimes. A website is my stronger side, where I can develop it and maintain it well.
  • No anonymous reports. I think we'll remove the anonymous thing. Citizens report problems with their names.
  • Photos and videos, no separate audio. Audio would not be that clear. Most of the videos will be of people talking in that video, so we can just remove audio as a separate upload.
  • Two ranking categories to start: locality and severity.
